## 2024-05-18 - Keypad Component Accessibility and Interaction Improvements
**Learning:** Interaction elements like numeric keypads (especially those custom-built, replacing native input keyboards on mobile) often lack important accessibility (a11y) and micro-UX features. Specifically, keys like 'DEL' (icon-only) and '.' (punctuation) can be confusing for screen readers if not explicitly labeled. Additionally, visual feedback (like `active:scale-95`) and clear focus states (`focus-visible:ring-2`) are crucial for reassuring users that their input was registered, especially on touch devices where tactile feedback might be minimal or absent, and for users navigating via keyboard. Utilizing `touch-manipulation` improves responsiveness by preventing double-tap-to-zoom delays on mobile.
**Action:** Always ensure that custom keypads or grids of buttons have appropriate `aria-label`s for non-alphanumeric or icon-only keys. Consistently apply a suite of interaction classes—such as `rounded-2xl` (for consistent styling), `touch-manipulation`, `active:scale-95` (for tactile feedback), `focus-visible:ring-2`, `focus-visible:ring-primary`, and `focus:outline-none` (for clear keyboard navigation focus states)—to all interactive button elements to maintain a high standard of accessibility and micro-UX across the application.
