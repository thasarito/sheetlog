## 2024-04-08 - Keypad Interaction & Accessibility\n**Learning:** Large, frequently tapped interactive elements like keypad numbers and special buttons require robust physical-like feedback (e.g., `active:scale-95`, `touch-manipulation`) and explicit accessibility labels (`aria-label`) since visual symbols like 'DEL' or '.' might not be read out meaningfully by screen readers.\n**Action:** Always verify keyboard focus (`focus-visible:ring-2`), active tactile states (`active:scale-95`), and screen reader clarity (`aria-label`) for custom numeric pads or grid buttons.
