## 2024-05-14 - Keypad accessibility and UX improvements
**Learning:** In a mobile-first app, custom keypads are frequent interaction points. Without ARIA labels on icon buttons (like "Delete") and symbols (like "Decimal point"), screen readers struggle. Further, standard tactile feedback (`active:scale-95`), increased hit areas (often tied to `touch-manipulation`), and clear focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-2`, `focus-visible:ring-primary`) are crucial for quick data entry and keyboard/switch accessibility.
**Action:** When auditing or building custom numeric entry or frequent interaction grids, always ensure non-text buttons have descriptive `aria-label`s, add `touch-manipulation` to prevent zoom delays, use `active:scale-95` for immediate tactile feedback, and ensure strong `focus-visible` styles for keyboard navigation.
