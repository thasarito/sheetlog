## 2024-04-16 - Accessible Keypad Interactions\n**Learning:** The Keypad component previously lacked proper ARIA labels for non-alphanumeric keys (`.` and `DEL`) and lacked critical micro-interactions like focus rings for keyboard navigation and touch feedback for mobile users.\n**Action:** Applied standard Tailwind utility classes (`active:scale-95`, `touch-manipulation`, `focus-visible:ring-2`, `focus-visible:ring-primary`, `focus:outline-none`) and explicitly mapped `aria-label` attributes to ensure consistent accessibility and interaction patterns across the keypad interface.
